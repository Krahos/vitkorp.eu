Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy applies to the Shuftle app, a collection of classical card games.
The person responsible for it is Vitkorp di Sebastiano Giordano. See the
[Contacts](@/contacts.md) page for how to reach me.

## Summary
Shuftle does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## What the app does with your data
- It has no accounts, no sign-in and no online features.
- It does not connect to the internet.
- It does not ask for any permission on your device.
- It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and no third-party services.
- Nothing about you or your games leaves your device.

## Children
Because the app collects no data, it can be used by players of any age.

## Distribution
The app is distributed through stores such as Google Play. Those stores may
process data about you when you download or buy the app, under their own
privacy policies. I don't receive that data.

## Changes
If a future version of Shuftle collects any data, for example to add accounts
or online play, this policy will be updated before that version is released,
and the date above will change.

## Contact
For any question about this policy, see the [Contacts](@/contacts.md) page.
